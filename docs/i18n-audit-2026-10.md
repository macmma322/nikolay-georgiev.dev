# Bilingual content audit, October 2026

An editorial pass over the English and Bulgarian text of the site: the dictionaries
(`src/i18n/ui/`), the data files (`src/data/`), the case studies (`src/content/projects/`,
`src/content/lab.json`), the templates and the Bulgarian blueprint guide (`blueprint/SETUP.bg.md`).
Facts, numbers, statuses and claims are unchanged. Trivial punctuation fixes are not listed.
Terminology decisions are recorded in [`i18n-glossary.md`](i18n-glossary.md).

## Overall finding

The Bulgarian was in better shape than expected: most of it reads as written by a developer,
with correct terminology and natural word order. The problems were concentrated in a few
places: the hero and SEO lines (calques such as „платформи в продукция“), the /setup page
(status labels, „къде не стига“, anglicisms such as „ъпгрейд“ and „хост на модели“), button
imperatives that switched between formal and informal address, and **untranslated fallbacks**:
the credit names, authors and licences on the Open source page and in every case study's
"Built with" block were English strings shown on Bulgarian pages. The English needed only a
handful of fixes.

## Uncertain terminology

- **bootloader → „зареждащ модул“.** Used consistently in Malrynth (13 places, one diagram
  label). It can be misread as a loadable kernel module; „програма за начално зареждане“ is the
  precise term but long for labels. Kept; owner to decide.
- **production status → „В експлоатация“.** Some Bulgarian developers prefer „В продукция“ as
  established jargon. Chosen for clarity to non-developers; easy to switch in `src/lib/status.ts`
  (and `npm run og` to refresh the card).
- **„Налично, не се използва“.** Short enough for the pill; „Притежавам го, но засега не го
  използвам“ (owner's suggestion) reads better in prose but does not fit the label and is gendered.
- **„Full-stack платформи“, „pull request-и“.** Latin-script loanwords as Bulgarian developers write
  them; no settled Cyrillic form.
- **„сътрудници“ for open-source contributors.** Alternatives: „участници“, „допринесли“.
- **„Техника“** for the /setup page (nav, test fixtures and the other agent's work depend on it):
  kept; „Оборудване“ would be the alternative.

## Checks added

- `scripts/check-i18n.mjs` (`npm run check:i18n`, first step of `npm run build`): walks every
  `{ en, bg }` leaf in `src/i18n/ui`, `src/data`, the diagrams, `src/lib/status.ts` and
  `src/content/lab.json`, plus each line of the Bulgarian case studies, and fails on an empty
  Bulgarian side, Bulgarian text that reads as English, `{placeholder}` sets or plural forms
  that differ, function leaves of different arity, and hardcoded `aria-label`, `alt`, `title`,
  `placeholder` or `aria-description` literals in `.astro` templates.
- `scripts/audit-dist.mjs`: built Bulgarian pages fail on an English sentence in visible text or
  in an accessible name, outside `<code>`, `<pre>`, `<kbd>`, `<samp>` and elements that declare
  `lang="en"`. It caught the 33 untranslated credit strings above. The existing checks already
  covered locale routing (a page linking to the other locale's route), unresolved values and
  Cyrillic on English pages.
- Missing keys are impossible by construction: `L<T>` requires both locales at type-check time,
  and `src/lib/projects.ts` checks that every Bulgarian case-study list has the English shape.
