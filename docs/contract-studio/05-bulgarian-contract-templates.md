# Bulgarian contract templates (v0.1.0, unreviewed)

Status: every template carries `"reviewStatus": "unreviewed"`. No text here has been approved by a lawyer, and nothing here guarantees that a clause is valid or enforceable. Research date: 2026-11-09.

## 1. What exists

Location: `contract-studio/templates/<key>.<lang>.json`, languages `bg` and `en`, identical section and clause keys per pair.

| key                    | category               | document                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `software-development` | `software_development` | Software development agreement (12 sections)                                                               |
| `it-services`          | `it_services`          | IT services agreement                                                                                      |
| `technical-annex`      | `annex`                | Annex - technical specification                                                                            |
| `additional-agreement` | `additional_agreement` | Additional agreement (scope or price change)                                                               |
| `acceptance-protocol`  | `acceptance_protocol`  | Acceptance protocol (clauses for accepted / accepted with reservations / rejected are `optional`; use one) |
| `payment-settlement`   | `payment_settlement`   | Payment or debt settlement agreement                                                                       |

Design facts:

- Contractor is a natural person. No EOOD wording, no VAT wording, no tax outcome asserted. Clause `price.taxes` only says each party bears the obligations the law places on it, and that the contract contains no tax calculation.
- Currency is EUR everywhere. `terms.price` and `settlement.amount` should hold the number only; the clause text adds "EUR".
- `terms.price_basis` is free wording (for example "gross"); no clause states a net/gross or tax result.
- Article numbering, the parties block and the signature block are left to the application.
- Only the whitelisted placeholders and list names are used. `acceptance-protocol` reuses `change.description` for the list of reservations (option B), because no dedicated placeholder exists.
- Acceptance by silence (`acceptance.silence`), IP conditioned on full payment (`ip.full-payment`) and the liability carve-out (`liability.no-exclusion`) are marked `material`.

## 4. What each law was checked for

- ZZD: structure of the contract for work (arts. 258-269) and mandate (arts. 280-287); acceptance (art. 264); defects (art. 265); client withdrawal (art. 268); penalty (art. 92); limits on excluding liability (art. 94(1) read in full: intent and gross negligence cannot be excluded or limited in advance); amendment by mutual consent (art. 20a). Statutory default interest and any provision on the form of acceptance were not read.
- ZAPSP: software is protected as a literary work (art. 3); author is the default owner of a commissioned work (art. 42(1)); exclusive rights need an express written grant (art. 36); employment rule for software (art. 14, partial). No program-specific writing requirement other than art. 36 was found in the text read. Moral rights provisions were not read.
- GDPR / Personal Data Protection Act: only art. 28 GDPR (controller-processor contract) was read. The Bulgarian Personal Data Protection Act (ЗЗЛД), the Commission for Personal Data Protection guidance (cpdp.bg) and the household exemption in art. 2(2)(c) were not read.
- Electronic documents and signatures: the Electronic Document and Electronic Trust Services Act (ЗЕДЕУУ) and Regulation (EU) 910/2014 (eIDAS, amended by 2024/1183) were not retrieved as primary text. Only a search-result snippet attributed to ЗЕДЕУУ art. 3(2) (written form satisfied by an electronic document containing an electronic statement) and art. 1(2) (law does not apply to transactions requiring qualified written form) was seen; the clause `general.electronic` therefore rests on unverified material.
- Tax and social security: see section 5(d). Nothing is encoded in the templates.
- Euro: confirmed from the European Commission page above.

## 5. Risk notes

(a) A civil contract is not suitable for every recurring business activity. Repeated, organised, paid activity for several clients may require business or freelance registration, and a civil contract with a single client that works like employment (fixed hours, subordination, tools and place set by the client, continuity) risks reclassification as an employment relationship. The clause `performance.independence` in `it-services` is a declaration only and does not change the facts. The legal tests, thresholds and consequences (including social-security and labour-inspection consequences, kzld.bg, nap.bg) were not verified in this research and need a lawyer or accountant.

(b) Full-time employer. Before any client project, check the employment contract and internal policies for: IP clauses (ZAPSP art. 14 contains a rule on software and databases created in an employment relationship; its full text and its limits were not read), exclusivity, non-compete, a duty to obtain consent for other paid work, conflict-of-interest and confidentiality rules, and use of employer equipment or time. If the employer may own or claim the work, a clause in a client contract granting rights cannot override that.

(c) Enforceability is not guaranteed. All templates are unreviewed. Wording on silence as acceptance, conditioning rights on full payment, liability caps, penalties and acceleration may be limited or struck by a court, and consumer or other mandatory rules may apply to some clients (not examined).

(d) Tax and social filing is separate from drafting. The templates contain no tax or contribution calculation and assert no tax result. Who withholds advance tax, which expense deduction applies, whether social-security contributions arise, and whether declarations (Образец 1, Образец 6) or a statement of amounts paid are due, depend on how the income is classified under the ZDDFL, КСО and ДОПК. The ZDDFL text read shows art. 42 covers employment income, so the common assumption that art. 42 governs civil contracts was not confirmed. The 25% figure and "current value" option were not confirmed for civil contracts. Obtain this from an accountant or NAP (nap.bg).

## 7. Open questions for a qualified Bulgarian lawyer

1. Characterisation: is `it-services` best treated as a mandate (ZZD arts. 280 ff.), a contract for work, or a mixed contract, and which default rules (for example termination under art. 287, remuneration under art. 286) override the drafted clauses?
2. ZZD art. 264(2): confirm the exact text on acceptance, silence and deemed acceptance, and whether `acceptance.silence` (deemed acceptance on expiry of `terms.acceptance_days` without a reasoned objection) is consistent with it and enforceable against the client.
3. ZZD art. 265 remedies and limitation periods (the text read mentions six months): how do they interact with `warranty.defects` and `terms.warranty_days`?
4. ZZD art. 268: the client's withdrawal right as read includes payment for work done, expenses and lost profit. Is `termination.client` (payment for work done and costs only) a valid contractual deviation?
5. ZAPSP art. 14: full text, paragraph structure, and effect on a full-time employee who works for a client outside the employment; how should the employment contract be reviewed?
6. ZAPSP art. 36 and any other provision: can a transfer or grant of copyright be made conditional on full payment (`ip.full-payment`), and does a "licence only" model need any form beyond writing? Is a "full assignment" model of computer-program rights possible in the form used, given art. 37 on future works and the author's non-transferable moral rights (not read)?
7. Statutory default interest for late payment: correct legal basis and whether a contractual penalty (ZZD art. 92) should replace or supplement `price.late` and `payment.late`; how a court may reduce an excessive penalty.
8. `liability.cap` and `liability.general` (exclusion of lost profit and indirect damage, data loss): are they valid within ZZD art. 94(1) and any other limits?
9. Electronic signature: effect of simple, advanced and qualified electronic signatures for these documents under ЗЕДЕУУ and eIDAS, and whether `general.electronic` and email notices are sufficient.
10. Personal data: when is the developer a processor, when a controller, when is a data processing agreement mandatory, and what does the Bulgarian Personal Data Protection Act add? Is a template DPA needed?
11. Tax and social security of civil-contract income for a natural person with a main employment: classification under the ZDDFL, who withholds, the applicable expense deduction, contributions under КСО, filing of Образец 1 and Образец 6, and a statement of amounts paid; and how a contract should be worded so it does not prejudge these (clause `price.taxes`).
12. Thresholds where repeated work requires registration or creates reclassification risk (see 5(a)), and what the contract and invoicing practice should look like to stay on the right side.
13. Dispute forum: wording for `terms.dispute_forum` (court or arbitration, venue) and any mandatory jurisdiction rules.
14. Client types: whether the templates need variants for consumers, sole traders and companies, and for non-Bulgarian clients (governing law, English version prevailing or not).
15. `payment-settlement`: legal effect of the acknowledgement of debt (for example on limitation) and of the acceleration clause.
