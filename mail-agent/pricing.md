- 3 rounds of design revisions.

### 4. Web platform or web app: from €5,000

No fixed price for 3D, custom dashboards, logins and accounts, or a full
online shop until the technical scoping is done.

## How a project runs

1. **Intro call (free, 30 min, Google Meet).** Goals, scope and questions.
   Booked online for a weekday lunchtime or evening, or a Saturday morning
   (Sofia time). It is not a technical scoping
   session: for web platforms that is paid discovery, arranged separately.
2. **Written scope and fixed quote,** usually within 2 working days of the
   call.
3. **Deposit:** 50% to book the project and 50% before launch. Larger
   projects are paid in milestones instead.
4. **Design** of the key pages for desktop and mobile, with the included
   revision rounds. One revision round is one combined set of feedback, not
   unlimited redesigns.
5. **Build** on a private preview link the client can check at any time.
6. **Launch,** then the 30-day bug-fix warranty and a handover.

Changes after the design is approved are quoted separately, at the hourly
rate or as a small fixed add-on.

## Design directions the client can pick from

- **Minimal and editorial:** lots of white space, strong type, few colours.
- **Bold and colourful:** large type, brand colours, playful sections.
- **Technical and dark:** like nikolay-georgiev.dev, precise and
  engineering-led.
- **Warm local business:** friendly photos, clear prices, easy contact.
- **Premium:** restrained palette, refined type, slow and elegant motion.

Motion level: none, subtle (gentle fades and hovers), or expressive
(scroll storytelling, 3D). Expressive motion fits the Custom tier.

## What to ask the client

Ask only the questions they have not already answered, at most six, most
useful first:

1. What does the business do, and what should the website achieve (calls,
   bookings, sales, trust, hiring)?
2. Which pages or sections do they need?
3. Two or three websites they like, and what exactly they like about each
   (layout, colours, photos, animation, tone). One they dislike, if any.
4. Do they already have a logo, brand colours, fonts, photos and text?
5. Features: contact form, booking, online shop, blog, editable content,
   languages (English, Bulgarian, other)?
6. Do they already have a domain and hosting, or an old site to replace?
7. When do they need it live, and is there a fixed date?
8. What budget range do they have in mind?
